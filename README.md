## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science undergraduate (6th semester)** who builds hands-on, working agentic AI systems rather than just studying them in theory. Currently training under **Panaversity's Agent Factory (PIAIC)** — Batch 72 — on the spec-driven, human-supervised curriculum for manufacturing **Digital FTEs** (AI Workers built to replace or augment a human Full-Time Equivalent role), heading toward the vendor-neutral **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** track.

I'm terminal-first, with **Claude Code** as my primary build agent — spec-driven development end-to-end, not just AI-assisted autocomplete.

## 🛠️ What I'm Actually Building

> Real, linked, working — not just talked about.

- 🤖 **[OpenClaw — WhatsApp Agentic Gateway](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad)** — wired Claude as the reasoning layer for a WhatsApp gateway that autonomously selects and invokes tools; deployed as a persistent `systemd` service on Ubuntu.
- 🦾 **[Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics — Interactive Textbook](https://muhammad-junaid-sajjad.github.io/Hackathon1/)** — solo-built, live Docusaurus + React + TypeScript platform: 4 modules, 12 chapters, 87+ sections covering ROS 2, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Gazebo, and VLA robot control. [Repo →](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad/Hackathon1)
- 🗳️ **[LGU MUN 2026 — Delegate Registration Platform](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad/LguMun)** — FastAPI + PostgreSQL, concurrency-safe registration (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`), load-tested for 450 concurrent users, 98% Playwright E2E pass rate.
- 📄 **[SDD-RI Research](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad/AI-Spec-Driven-Development)** — self-published paper (IEEE citation format, not peer-reviewed) introducing Specification-Driven Development with Recursive Intelligence.
- 🩺 **[Chronic Kidney Disease Prediction (ML)](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad/CCP_ML_Theory)** — academic group project; independently led preprocessing, feature selection, and model comparison.
- ⚙️ **[Hybrid CPU](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad/Ai-based--Hybrid-architectural-project-) & [RISC Simulator](https://github.com/Muhammad-Junaid-Sajjad/Mips_Project_01)** — two interactive CPU simulators in vanilla JS, including a custom instruction-fusion design.
